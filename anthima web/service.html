Recycle is

"Recycling is the process of collecting and processing materials that would otherwise be thrown away as trash and turning them in to new products."

Benefits of recycling

Conserves our natural resources

protect eco-system and wildlife

Recycling Help environment

Recycling also reduces the need for extracting, refining and processing raw materials all of which create substantial air and water.

Recycling reduce pollution

Recycling saves energy and water, it also indirectly lowers pollution by reducing contaminated water and pollutants produced during energy production.

If do not recycle

When we do not recycle is substantially increase waste, depleted natural resources, pollution of the environent around the globe, negative social impacts.

Three recycle words

Reduce, Reuse, Recycle- these three 'R' words are an important part of sustainable living, as they help to cut down on the amount of waste we have to throw away.
